toklab

LLM tokenizers written from scratch in Python, simulated step by step.

Starting… GitHub

Type some text and watch it travel through the tokenizer pipeline: normalization, pre-tokenization, bytes, sub-word merges and finally the ids a model sees.

Train a sub-word tokenizer on a corpus and replay every merge. BPE merges the most frequent pair; WordPiece merges the pair with the best likelihood score count(ab) / (count(a)·count(b)).

Training corpus (editable)

The same text under four tokenizers. Word-level needs <unk> for anything unseen, WordPiece only fails on unseen characters, and byte-level BPE never fails. GPT-2's 50k vocabulary gives the shortest sequences.

All tokenization runs in your browser: the page loads toklab's Python source into Pyodide. The word, WordPiece and BPE models are trained on a small sample corpus when the page opens; GPT-2 uses OpenAI's published vocabulary.
